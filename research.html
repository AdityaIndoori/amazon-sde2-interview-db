Clarity behind every count.

Count reports, not repetitions

Frequency is the number of distinct candidate reports for a question in a particular round. Cross-posts and repeated mentions do not add to the count. It is not an estimate of Amazon’s asking rate.

Preserve the date evidence

Interview dates take precedence. When unavailable, publication dates are explicitly labeled. Month- and year-only dates retain that precision; no exact interview day is inferred.

Keep the scope narrow

External SDE II / L5 final loops only. Round verified requires a supported position in rounds 1–4; Round unconfirmed verifies the final-loop stage but leaves the ordinal unknown. Online assessments, phone screens, internal transfers, and other levels are excluded. Locations are countries only; “Not stated” means the report does not establish a country.

Scoped research campaigns

A ledger of documented research activity, not exhaustive interview coverage. Country and dates describe the search scope, never an interview’s location or date. These controls are independent of question filters.

Using the question filters

Select multiple values using checkboxes. Values within a filter match any selection; different filters must all match. No selections means all values. Year, country, date basis, and evidence quality must match the same occurrence; counts, badges, practice links, and exports update accordingly. “Exact named” requires curator-backed source evidence; “Described” means a reported description, not a completeness guarantee; “Partial” preserves unspecified variants. Quality does not establish the date or round.

Round verified requires a supported ordinal. Round unconfirmed leaves that ordinal unknown. All shows both collections without merging row frequencies; shared reports count once. Numbered round filters exclude unconfirmed rows. Bookmarks remain attached to their original collection.
