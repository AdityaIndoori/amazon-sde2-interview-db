PROVENANCE FIRST

What the numbers mean.

Count reports, not repetitions

Frequency is the number of distinct candidate reports for a question in a particular round. Cross-posts and repeated mentions do not add to the count. It is not an estimate of Amazon’s asking rate.

Preserve the date evidence

Interview dates take precedence. When unavailable, publication dates are explicitly labeled. Month- and year-only dates retain that precision; no exact interview day is inferred.

Keep the scope narrow

External SDE II / L5 final loops, rounds 1–4 only. Online assessments, phone screens, internal transfers, and other levels are excluded. Explicit OA/screen numbering offsets are documented in the evidence. “Not stated” means the report supplied no location.

Search coverage, exclusions & limitations

Coverage reflects the searches documented below, not a complete inventory of Amazon interviews. Source access gaps and ambiguous reports remain visible rather than being filled in.
